After putting real money in the market for several months, I have gained some insights and want to put them down below. 

From March 11 to July 16, I invested in several stocks, including AMD, Qualcomn, Nike, Tencent and Midea. I deposited 7,166CAD in total, and now I have a total value of 8,176CAD.

After reflecting the decisions I made, I found that:

## 1. Be able to hold stocks after a rise.
*拿住股票是关键*

When buying long, a good company can double or triple in a few years. Even some of your choices go wrong, the overall benefit of holding is still positive.

## 2. Build your own invest philosophy/plan.
*要有自己的交易策略*

If you don't have your own trading philosophy, you will be swayed by others or the market easily.
